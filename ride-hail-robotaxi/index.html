About this analysis

This is an independent analysis by Carl Fredlund, researched and visualized with the help of agentic AI. Every app, owner, city, and robotaxi status was checked against public sources where they exist, and the sources are listed above. Even so, it covers more than 200 urban areas and over 100 apps, and some local ground truths were hard to confirm. Treat it as a well-sourced overview, not a definitive record.

Download figures in particular rest on assumptions. Google Play publishes only install ranges, Apple publishes no install counts, and Chinese and Iranian Android stores report differently. Where a figure was not public, it is a best estimate from the information available and is marked with "~" or a striped bar. Rankings and shares should be read as approximate.

Not affiliated with any company shown or with my employer. Corrections are welcome.

Badges are colored monograms, not official logos. Urban-area populations: Demographia World Urban Areas (2025), with local estimates for a few cities. Robotaxi service areas cover only part of each metro. Base map: Natural Earth.
